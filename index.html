ELITE PLAYMAKERS FC - TOURNAMENT RULES

Strict adherence to rules is required for all participating players.

1. Match Settings

  • Match Duration: 8 Minutes
  • Extra Time: OFF (Group Stage) / ON (Knockout Stage)
  • Penalties: OFF (Group Stage) / ON (Knockout Stage)
  • Condition: Excellent / Normal
  • Injuries: OFF

2. Screenshot & Result Verification

Both players must take a full-screen screenshot showing the final scoreboard and match statistics. The winner must send the screenshot to the designated WhatsApp group or management line immediately after the match.

3. Network Disconnection Policy

If a disconnection occurs in the 1st half before 30 mins, restart the match and play the remaining time. If disconnected after 70 mins with a clear goal difference, management reserves the right to decide the final result.

4. Fair Play & Conduct

Abusive language, toxic behavior, or deliberate time-wasting back-passing will result in immediate disqualification without warning.
